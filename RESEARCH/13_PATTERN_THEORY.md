# Purpose

This document investigates one of the central hypotheses of Project BRAHMA:

> **Reality appears to organize itself through recurring patterns rather than complete randomness.**

The goal is to investigate whether these patterns represent universal organizational principles shared across mathematics, physics, biology, artificial intelligence, music, neuroscience, and human cognition.

---

# Scope

This document studies patterns as a scientific phenomenon.

It does not assume that all observed similarities imply causation.

Instead, it investigates whether recurring structures can be mathematically modeled and experimentally verified.

---

# Core Research Questions

- What is a pattern?
- Why do patterns emerge?
- Why does nature prefer ordered structures?
- Can intelligence be defined as pattern recognition?
- Can consciousness be represented through dynamic patterns?
- Can universal patterns be mathematically described?
