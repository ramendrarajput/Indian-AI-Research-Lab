# Purpose

This document identifies the deepest unanswered questions that have shaped human civilization across science, philosophy, mathematics, artificial intelligence, and spirituality.

Its purpose is not to answer these questions immediately, but to organize them into a structured research framework that guides future investigations within Project BRAHMA.

---

# Scope

This document focuses only on **fundamental questions** whose answers have the potential to transform multiple disciplines simultaneously.

It intentionally excludes detailed theories, mathematical derivations, or speculative conclusions.

Those belong in their respective research documents.

---

# Core Research Questions

- Why does anything exist?
- What is reality?
- Why does nature follow patterns?
- What is information?
- What is intelligence?
- What is consciousness?
- What is life?
- What is space?
- What is time?
- Can mathematics completely describe reality?
