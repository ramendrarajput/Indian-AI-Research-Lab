# Purpose

This document presents the central scientific hypothesis of Project BRAHMA.

Rather than proposing isolated theories, Project BRAHMA seeks to unify observations from mathematics, information, intelligence, consciousness, physics, biology, music, and philosophy into a single evolving research framework.

The BRAHMA Hypothesis is not presented as absolute truth.

It is a scientific proposal designed to be explored, challenged, refined, or even replaced through evidence and future discoveries.
