# Purpose

This document investigates one of the central questions of Project BRAHMA:

> **Is intelligence an emergent property of organized information?**

The primary objective is to understand whether information, when organized according to universal principles, naturally gives rise to intelligence.

Project BRAHMA explores this question by integrating concepts from Information Theory, Artificial Intelligence, Neuroscience, Mathematics, Physics, and Cognitive Science.

---

# Scope

This document focuses on intelligence as an information-processing phenomenon.

It investigates:

- Information
- Knowledge
- Intelligence
- Learning
- Memory
- Prediction
- Decision Making

This document intentionally excludes consciousness as an independent phenomenon, which is investigated separately.

---

# Core Research Questions

- What is information?
- What differentiates data from information?
- What differentiates information from knowledge?
- What differentiates knowledge from intelligence?
- Can intelligence emerge from information alone?
- Can intelligence exist without learning?
- Can intelligence exist without prediction?
- Can intelligence be mathematically measured?
