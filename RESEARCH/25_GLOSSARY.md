# Project BRAHMA Glossary

## Official Research Vocabulary

> **Version 1.0**
>
> This glossary defines the official vocabulary used throughout Project BRAHMA.
>
> Every important term has exactly one canonical definition inside the project.
>
> These definitions may evolve as research progresses, but every revision must preserve conceptual consistency.
>
> **Purpose:** Build a common language before building a common theory.

---

# A

## Adaptation

The ability of a system to modify its internal behaviour in response to changing environmental conditions while maintaining functionality.

Related:
Learning • Intelligence • Evolution

---

# B

## Behaviour

The observable actions or responses produced by a system as a consequence of internal processing.

Behaviour is considered an output rather than intelligence itself.

---

# C

## Cognition

The collection of processes through which an intelligent system acquires, processes, stores, retrieves and applies information.

---

## Consciousness

Within Project BRAHMA, consciousness refers to the subjective aspect of awareness whose fundamental nature remains scientifically unresolved.

Project BRAHMA does not assume any particular theory of consciousness.

Instead, consciousness is treated as an open scientific research problem.

---

## Complexity

The emergence of behaviour that cannot be completely understood by examining individual components independently.

---

## Computation

The transformation of information according to defined rules.

Both biological and artificial systems perform computation.

---

# E

## Emergence

The appearance of higher-level properties resulting from interactions among lower-level components.

Emergence is considered one of the central research concepts within Project BRAHMA.

---

## Evidence

Objectively verifiable observations supporting or contradicting a hypothesis.

Project BRAHMA distinguishes evidence from belief.

---

# H

## Hierarchy

An organization in which simpler structures combine to produce increasingly complex structures.

Hierarchy is one of the foundational concepts investigated by Project BRAHMA.

---

## Hypothesis

A proposed explanation that remains open to testing and revision.

A hypothesis is never treated as established knowledge without supporting evidence.

---

# I

## Information

Any distinguishable pattern capable of reducing uncertainty within an intelligent system.

Information may exist independently of the medium through which it is represented.

---

## Intelligence

The capacity of a system to acquire, organize, transform and apply information toward adaptive goals.

Project BRAHMA intentionally separates intelligence from consciousness.

---

# K

## Knowledge

Organized information that has demonstrated reliability through observation, reasoning or experimentation.

---

# L

## Learning

The process through which a system improves future behaviour by incorporating experience into its internal organization.

---

# M

## Mathematics

The formal language used to describe logical relationships, quantities, structures and transformations.

Project BRAHMA investigates whether mathematics merely describes reality or reflects deeper organizational principles.

---

## Model

A simplified representation of reality constructed for explanation, prediction or experimentation.

---

# O

## Observation

A recorded phenomenon obtained through measurement, perception or experimentation.

Observations precede hypotheses.

---

# P

## Pattern

A recurring structure, relationship or organization observed across one or more systems.

Patterns may exist spatially, temporally, mathematically or informationally.

Project BRAHMA considers pattern recognition to be one of the fundamental tools of interdisciplinary research.

---

## Pattern Hierarchy

A multi-layer organization in which simple patterns combine to generate increasingly complex patterns.

This concept represents one of the foundational hypotheses of Project BRAHMA.

---

# R

## Reality

The totality of existence independent of individual interpretation.

Project BRAHMA makes no assumptions regarding whether reality is fundamentally physical, informational or otherwise.

---

## Research

The disciplined process of generating, evaluating and refining knowledge through observation, mathematics and experimentation.

---

# S

## Synchronization

The process through which two or more systems become increasingly coordinated in behaviour, timing or organization.

Project BRAHMA investigates synchronization as a possible universal organizational principle.

---

## Systems

Collections of interacting components whose collective behaviour differs from the behaviour of individual parts.

---

# T

## Theory

A coherent explanatory framework supported by substantial evidence.

Project BRAHMA distinguishes theory from hypothesis.

---

# U

## Universe

The complete physical reality within which all observable phenomena occur.

Project BRAHMA remains scientifically neutral regarding metaphysical interpretations of the universe.

---

# Universal Pattern

A recurring organizational principle appearing across multiple independent scientific domains.

The existence of a universal pattern requires evidence across disciplines rather than isolated similarity.

---

# V

## Validation

The process of determining whether observations support or contradict a proposed hypothesis.

---

# Working Principle

A conceptual assumption adopted temporarily to guide investigation while awaiting further evidence.

Working principles remain revisable.

---

# Core Principles

Throughout Project BRAHMA, the following distinctions remain fundamental.

Observation ≠ Interpretation

Interpretation ≠ Hypothesis

Hypothesis ≠ Theory

Theory ≠ Fact

Similarity ≠ Causation

Analogy ≠ Evidence

Belief ≠ Scientific Validation

---

# BRAHMA Vocabulary Rules

1. Every important term has one official definition.

2. The same word should never carry multiple meanings within the project.

3. Scientific terminology takes precedence over metaphorical language.

4. Philosophical concepts may inspire hypotheses but cannot replace evidence.

5. Definitions evolve through evidence, never through preference.

---
