# Purpose

This document investigates one of the oldest scientific mysteries:

> **Why does mathematics describe nature so extraordinarily well?**

Project BRAHMA explores whether mathematics is merely a human invention or the fundamental language through which reality organizes itself.

---

# Scope

This document investigates mathematical structures appearing across:

- Nature
- Physics
- Biology
- Music
- Artificial Intelligence
- Human Cognition

The objective is to identify universal mathematical principles underlying recurring patterns.

---

# Core Research Questions

- Why is mathematics universal?
- Is mathematics invented or discovered?
- Why do natural systems follow mathematical laws?
- Can every pattern be mathematically represented?
- Can intelligence be expressed mathematically?
- Can consciousness eventually be described mathematically?
