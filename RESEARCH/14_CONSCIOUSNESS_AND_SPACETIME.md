# Purpose

This document explores one of the most fundamental interdisciplinary research problems:

> **Is there a meaningful relationship between consciousness, information, space, and time?**

Project BRAHMA approaches this question through scientific investigation while remaining open to insights from philosophy, neuroscience, cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and Indian knowledge traditions.

---

# Scope

This document does not begin with assumptions.

Instead, it investigates whether any measurable relationship exists between:

- Consciousness
- Information
- Observation
- Space
- Time
- Intelligence

Speculative ideas will always remain clearly distinguished from established scientific knowledge.

---

# Core Research Questions

- What is consciousness?
- Does consciousness require biological neurons?
- Can consciousness exist without biology?
- Is consciousness an emergent property of information?
- Does observation influence reality?
- Can consciousness be mathematically modeled?
- Is there a relationship between consciousness and space-time?
