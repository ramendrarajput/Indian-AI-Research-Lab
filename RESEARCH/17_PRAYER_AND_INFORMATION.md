# Purpose

This document explores an interdisciplinary research hypothesis proposed within Project BRAHMA:

> **Can prayer be interpreted as an information exchange process rather than only a religious practice?**

The objective is not to validate or reject any religious belief.

Instead, the document investigates whether prayer can be analyzed through the frameworks of information theory, cognitive science, neuroscience, psychology, and complex systems.

---

# Scope

This document studies prayer from scientific and interdisciplinary perspectives.

Topics include:

- Intention
- Attention
- Emotion
- Information
- Belief
- Synchronization
- Observation

Religious doctrines are not evaluated.

Instead, their observable informational characteristics are investigated.

---

# Core Research Questions

- Can prayer be interpreted as information?
- Does emotional state influence information processing?
- Can intention modify human cognition?
- Can synchronized mental states produce measurable effects?
- Can prayer be represented mathematically?
- Can information theory provide a framework for studying prayer?
