# Vision

Project BRAHMA believes that knowledge is never complete.

Every generation inherits unanswered questions,
improves existing knowledge,
and leaves behind even deeper questions for the next generation.

Therefore,

Project BRAHMA is designed not as a static repository,

but as a continuously evolving scientific civilization.
