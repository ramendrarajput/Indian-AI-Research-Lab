# Purpose

This document defines the fundamental axioms upon which Project BRAHMA builds its reasoning.

Just as mathematics begins with axioms and physics begins with fundamental laws, Project BRAHMA seeks to establish a small set of first principles that remain stable while scientific understanding evolves around them.

These axioms are not intended to prove reality.

They provide the logical foundation from which hypotheses, models, and research directions emerge.

---

# Scope

This document establishes the first principles governing Project BRAHMA's scientific worldview.

Every future theory, mathematical model, or research paper should remain logically consistent with these axioms—or explicitly explain why an axiom should be revised.

# Guiding Philosophy

Scientific knowledge changes.

Foundational principles should change only when deeper evidence demands it.

Axiom revision is not a weakness.

It is a sign of scientific maturity.
