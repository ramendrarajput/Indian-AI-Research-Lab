# BRAHMA Research School

## An Open School of Infinite Questions

Project BRAHMA Research School shall remain permanently open to every curious mind, regardless of nationality, age, profession, academic degree, religion, language, or economic background.

Knowledge should never become the privilege of a few.

Just as the Sun illuminates every planet without discrimination,

Project BRAHMA shall illuminate every sincere researcher without expectation of ownership.

---

## The Principle of the Eternal Question

Project BRAHMA does not believe that humanity finally solves its deepest questions.

Instead,

every answer is considered a better approximation that gives birth to deeper questions.

Questions therefore never truly end.

They evolve.

Every generation inherits the same questions,

but explores them with better mathematics,

better instruments,

better intelligence,

and deeper understanding.

---

## Knowledge is Infinite

Reality is larger than every scientific model.

Therefore,

every theory inside Project BRAHMA remains open to revision.

No document shall ever be considered absolutely final.

No researcher shall claim complete truth.

The repository itself is designed as a living system that evolves with evidence.

---

## The Sun Principle

The Sun shines equally upon every world.

It does not choose who deserves light.

Project BRAHMA follows the same philosophy.

Knowledge belongs to humanity.

Questions belong to civilization.

Understanding belongs to whoever sincerely seeks it.

---

## The Responsibility of Every Researcher

Every researcher entering Project BRAHMA accepts one responsibility:

> Leave humanity with better questions than the ones you inherited.

Answers may become obsolete.

Questions create civilizations.

---

## The BRAHMA Principle

A solved question ends curiosity.

An evolving question creates new science.

Therefore,

Project BRAHMA preserves questions,

not because humanity is ignorant,

but because curiosity is infinite.

---

*"The purpose of science is not to end questions.

The purpose of science is to improve them."*

Project BRAHMA is an Open Research Civilization.
**— Project BRAHMA**
