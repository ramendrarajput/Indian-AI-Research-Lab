# Purpose

This document explores one of the long-term visions of Project BRAHMA:

> **Can a unified framework explain every known form of intelligence?**

Instead of treating biological intelligence, artificial intelligence, collective intelligence, and natural intelligence as isolated phenomena, Project BRAHMA investigates whether they emerge from the same underlying informational and mathematical principles.

---

# Scope

The document investigates intelligence across multiple domains:

- Biological Intelligence
- Artificial Intelligence
- Collective Intelligence
- Swarm Intelligence
- Evolutionary Intelligence
- Human Intelligence
- Machine Intelligence
- Universal Intelligence (Hypothetical)

This document intentionally avoids assuming the existence of a universal intelligence.

It investigates the possibility scientifically.

---

# Core Research Questions

- What is intelligence?
- Is intelligence substrate-independent?
- Can intelligence emerge from information?
- Can intelligence exist without consciousness?
- Is intelligence measurable?
- Can intelligence evolve naturally?
- Can different intelligences be unified mathematically?
- Is there a universal law governing intelligence?
