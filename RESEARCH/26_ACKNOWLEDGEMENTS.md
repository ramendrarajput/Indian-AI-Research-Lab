# Purpose

This document recognizes the individuals, civilizations, scientific communities, philosophical traditions, educational institutions, books, and open-source contributors whose work has directly or indirectly contributed to the evolution of Project BRAHMA.

Project BRAHMA believes that no meaningful discovery is created in isolation.

Every new idea is built upon countless ideas developed by previous generations.

This document exists to honor that intellectual inheritance.
