> *"Architecture is remembered through decisions.

---

# PURPOSE

The Decision Log serves as the permanent historical record of Project BRAHMA.

Every significant institutional, research, architectural, and engineering decision shall be documented here before or during its implementation.

The purpose of this document is not merely to record **what** was decided, but **why** it was decided.

Future contributors should be able to understand the reasoning behind every major decision without relying on memory or private discussions.

# PB-0001

### Title

Project BRAHMA is established as a Scientific Research Institution.

### Date

2026

### Category

Institutional

### Status

Accepted

### Context

The project evolved beyond an AI software application into a multidisciplinary scientific ecosystem.

### Decision

Project BRAHMA shall be developed as an open scientific and engineering institution rather than a conventional software project.

### Rationale

Scientific knowledge should outlive software implementations.

### Expected Impact

All future research, engineering, and documentation will follow institutional principles rather than product-centric development.

### Related Documents

* 01_VISION.md
* 02_PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md
