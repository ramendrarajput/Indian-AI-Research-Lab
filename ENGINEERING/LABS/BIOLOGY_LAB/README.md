> *"Life is not merely chemistry.

---

# Purpose

The Biology Laboratory is the laboratory of living systems within Project BRAHMA.

Its purpose is to study how matter organizes itself into life, how living systems process information, adapt to changing environments, evolve over time, and ultimately give rise to intelligence and consciousness.

Within Project BRAHMA, biology is regarded as the bridge between physical reality and living intelligence.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Biology Laboratory is to understand the fundamental principles governing life across all scales—from molecules and cells to ecosystems and civilizations.

The laboratory seeks not only to explain biological systems but also to discover universal principles of organization, adaptation, evolution, and emergence that may apply beyond biology itself.

---

# Philosophy

Project BRAHMA considers life to be one of the universe's greatest emergent phenomena.

Matter organizes.

Information accumulates.

Systems adapt.

Life emerges.

Life is therefore viewed as an evolving information-processing system capable of preserving, transforming, and transmitting knowledge across generations.

The Biology Laboratory studies these processes scientifically while remaining open to deeper questions regarding the origin and nature of life.

# Core Research Questions

The laboratory continuously investigates questions such as:

- What is life?
- How does life emerge from non-living matter?
- Why does evolution produce increasing complexity?
- Is DNA fundamentally an information system?
- Can life exist beyond Earth?
- Can artificial life be created?
- Why do biological systems self-organize?
- How does evolution generate intelligence?
- Can universal biological principles apply to artificial systems?

These questions remain permanently open.
