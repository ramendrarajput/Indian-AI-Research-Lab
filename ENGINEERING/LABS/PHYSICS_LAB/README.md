# PHYSICS LAB

> *"Physics does not create the laws of the universe.
It discovers the patterns through which the universe continuously transforms itself."*

---

# Purpose

The Physics Laboratory studies the fundamental laws governing matter, energy, space, time, motion, and the evolution of the universe.

Within Project BRAHMA, physics is regarded as the science that transforms mathematical structures into observable reality.

If Mathematics describes possibility,

Physics describes existence.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Physics Laboratory is to understand the physical mechanisms through which the universe operates and evolves.

The laboratory seeks to unify classical physics, quantum physics, relativity, information, and future theories into a coherent understanding of reality.

---

# Philosophy

Project BRAHMA views physics as the observable expression of mathematical order.

Mathematics defines relationships.

Physics reveals how those relationships manifest within space and time.

The universe is therefore understood as an evolving system governed by discoverable physical patterns.

# Core Research Questions

The laboratory continuously investigates questions such as:

- What is space?
- What is time?
- Why do physical laws exist?
- Why are physical constants stable?
- Can information be considered a physical entity?
- Is gravity fundamentally geometric or informational?
- Can quantum mechanics and relativity be unified?
- Does the universe fundamentally operate through patterns?
- Is reality computational?

These questions remain permanently open.
