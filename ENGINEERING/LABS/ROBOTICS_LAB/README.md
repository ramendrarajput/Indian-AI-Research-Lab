# Purpose

The Robotics Laboratory is the embodied intelligence laboratory of Project BRAHMA.

Its purpose is to design, build, and study intelligent physical systems capable of perceiving, reasoning, acting, learning, and collaborating within the real world.

Within Project BRAHMA, robotics is viewed as the convergence of mathematics, physics, artificial intelligence, computation, sensing, and mechanical engineering into autonomous systems that interact with reality.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Robotics Laboratory is to engineer intelligent machines capable of assisting humanity in scientific research, exploration, healthcare, education, industry, disaster response, and space exploration.

The laboratory seeks to develop robots that are not merely automated machines, but adaptive learning systems capable of understanding and interacting with complex environments.

---

# Philosophy

Project BRAHMA considers robotics to be the physical manifestation of intelligence.

Mathematics provides structure.

Physics provides the laws of motion.

Artificial Intelligence provides reasoning.

Quantum technologies may provide future computational power.

Robotics integrates all of these into systems capable of interacting with reality.

A robot is therefore understood as an intelligent information-processing system embodied within the physical universe.

# Core Research Questions

The laboratory continuously investigates questions such as:

- What makes a robot truly autonomous?
- Can robots develop curiosity?
- Can intelligence exist without a biological body?
- How should robots learn from experience?
- Can robots become scientific collaborators?
- What distinguishes intelligent behavior from programmed behavior?
- Can embodiment improve intelligence?
- How should robots interact safely with humans?
- Can robotic systems contribute to understanding consciousness?

These questions remain permanently open.
